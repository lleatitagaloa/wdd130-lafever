Welcome to my Project Page

Go Kart

This was a very fun project I did me and my friend sketched out and built a Go Kart from scratch. It was challenging it taught me a lot of skills and a lot more about the fabrication process.

Dune Buggy

This one was way more out of my depth, We wanted to take a snowmobile engine put it in a frame we built from the front and back half of a ATV, This was a grueling project but was by far the most rewarding, even if we didn't fully finish it.

Portable FPV Screen

My biggest hobby that I have is FPV Drones and this was a simple enough project basically take old goggles I didn't use and 3d model an enclosure to hold all the internals.

Portable A/V to HDMI

This was for my FPV setup again it was so I can take my goggle feed and put it on a bigger screen for others to watch what I see, but the problem was that it needed to be plugged into the wall so I added a battery and a charging circuit for it.

Model Car

This one I had this small RC drift car that I thought looked cool cause it was just a small model car and so I made it so when you plug it into power the LEDs get powered on without needing any of the other circuits.

F1 Steering Wheel

I basically took my old wheel from my racing simulator and modeled a F1 styled wheel for the G920 racing wheel. This was very tricky getting the holes lined up because they don't follow a pattern

LED Light TV Stand

I added led lights to my TV stand, but I cut the LEDs to length then I soldered wires to move the LEDs to the next shelf. It turned out really nice and was a very simple project just time-consuming cutting everything to length.

Portable Charger Holder

This was a very fast project to make, but it was a very satisfying one for some reason. I have 3 of the same type of portable charger and I needed a way to store them and so I made this design to fit on my pegboard.

FPV Drone

This is one of my drones that I have. This one was modified. It was a bunch of components I had laying around and so I threw it together, the funniest part is its one of my favorite drones to fly.
